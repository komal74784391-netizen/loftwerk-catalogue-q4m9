The QP-2 measures quiescent current on battery-powered assemblies from 100 nA to
2 A with automatic range switching. The design package contains the Rev 2.3
schematic, the production BOM, the measurement firmware and the calibration
procedure.

Bench notes and characterisation logs live in a separate scratchpad repository.
